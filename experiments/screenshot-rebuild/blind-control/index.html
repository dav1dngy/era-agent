THE FIELDWORK WAY

Nothing rushed.
Nothing overcomplicated.

We like coffee that makes you pause. The smell when you open the bag. The sound of the kettle. That first, very necessary sip.

Our approach is simple: small batches, thoughtful roasting, and room for each coffee’s character to shine. No complicated rules. Just find what you love, and make it part of your day.

A LITTLE MORNING GUIDANCE

Your next good cup.

A simple pour-over recipe.
A starting point, not a rulebook.

  1. 01

    Start fresh.

    Grind 20 g of whole-bean coffee to a medium texture. Rinse your paper filter and warm your mug.

  2. 02

    Let it bloom.

    Add 40 g of water just off the boil. Give it a gentle swirl and wait 30 seconds.

  3. 03

    Take it slow.

    Pour steadily to 320 g total water. Let it drain, give your cup a swirl, and enjoy.
